Photoartai-Photoeditor

Privacy Policy

Effective 22 September 2026

This policy explains how Photoartai-Photoeditor (“the app”) handles information when you use it on iPhone.

Information we collect

Photoartai-Photoeditor does not require an account and does not upload your photos or proofs to a server. We do not operate an app backend.

Looks, crops, and saved proofs stay in the app on this iPhone. If you pick a photo, the system photo picker gives the app a copy for that proof. Photoartai-Photoeditor does not write back to Photos and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Device access

The app does not use the camera. The system photo picker does not require a photo-library permission prompt. Nothing else on the device is read.

Information that may leave your device

Opening the Privacy Policy or Terms of Use loads this website from Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process limited technical request data, such as an IP address, to deliver the page. Photoartai-Photoeditor does not use that visit to identify you inside the app.

The support form opens your email app. Your email provider sends the message to our support address. The website does not store a copy.

Data retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you use Delete All Data in Settings or uninstall the app. We cannot restore it.

Children

Photoartai-Photoeditor is a general-audience utility. It is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above shows the latest revision.
